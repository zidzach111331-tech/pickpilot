PickPilot Rules & Regulations

1. Authorized Access & Time Expiration

Client passes are provisioned with custom expiration periods. Once your designated time limit elapses, account access will automatically lock, and credentials will be masked immediately.

2. Prohibition of Unauthorized Sharing

Access links are strictly single-user licenses. Redistributing, re-selling, or publicly posting your direct access link or exposed credentials will lead to immediate cancellation.

3. Data Integrity & Anti-Scraping

Automated scraping, bot querying, or bulk extraction tools against PickPilot directory listings are strictly prohibited.

4. Renewal & Support Policy

To extend your session or request a new active access window upon expiration, reach out directly to the Administrator.
